# Chunking Strategies and Configurable Chunking

## Goal

Build a configurable chunking engine that transforms normalized
documents into retrieval-ready chunks.

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this day, I should understand:

- Why chunking exists.
- Chunk size versus retrieval precision.
- Chunk overlap.
- Character-based chunking.
- Why naive chunking can destroy semantic meaning.
- Metadata propagation.
- Chunk identity.
- Why chunking strategy should be configurable.
